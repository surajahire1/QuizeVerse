Last updated: October 2026

At QuizVerse, accessible from https://quizverse.example.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by QuizVerse and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

QuizVerse does not require mandatory user registration or collection of personally identifiable information (PII) like national identity numbers or payment details to play quizzes. Any gameplay data such as high scores, streaks, and custom nicknames are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on your own device.

2. Log Files

QuizVerse follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, QuizVerse uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, language selection, light/dark theme preference, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Google is one of our third-party advertising vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to QuizVerse and other sites on the internet. QuizVerse's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.
